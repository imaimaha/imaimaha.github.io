How it works

  • No install and no sign-up. Just open a game and play.
  • The game screens are in Japanese. The one-line tips above each game are all you need to start.
  • The daily puzzles are the same as in the iPhone apps and change at midnight Japan time (15:00 UTC).
  • Your results are kept in this browser on this device only.

🌏 Good to know

  • Available on the App Store in Japan. We haven't decided yet whether to release the apps in other countries, so the App Store links may not open from a store in another country.
  • Menus and in-game text are in Japanese for now. Most games are easy to play without reading Japanese.
  • Daily puzzles change at midnight Japan time (15:00 UTC), the same for every player.
