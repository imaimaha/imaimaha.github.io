FAQ

Common questions about the games, languages and availability.

Are the apps free?

Yes. Every app is free to download and play, with no in-app purchases. Most games are supported by ads; each game's page explains where ads appear.

Can I download them outside Japan?

The apps are currently available on the App Store in Japan only. We haven't decided whether to release them in other countries yet.

If your Apple Account uses a different country's App Store, the App Store links on this site may not open the app.

Are the games in English?

Not yet. Menus and in-game text are in Japanese for now. Most of the games are easy to play without reading Japanese, and each game page on this site explains the rules in English.

When does the daily puzzle change?

Every day at midnight Japan time (JST, UTC+9) - that is 15:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same puzzle that day.

Do I need an account to join the rankings?

No. Just choose a nickname. No sign-in or email address is needed, and you can delete your nickname and records from the ranking screen at any time.

Can I play without installing anything?

Yes. Several daily puzzles (and a match against the CPU in Gekokujo Duel) can be played in your web browser, with no sign-up. The game screens are in Japanese. See Play in your browser.

Where is my data stored?

Game records are basically saved on your iPhone. If you join a ranking, only your nickname and records are sent.

Will I get notifications?

Some games with a daily puzzle ask whether you'd like an optional daily reminder at 7 pm. You can turn it off any time in Settings.

Is there an Android version?

No, the apps are for iPhone only.

How can I contact you?

Send an email to imaimaha.apps@gmail.com. English or Japanese is fine. I make the apps alone, so a reply may take a little time.
