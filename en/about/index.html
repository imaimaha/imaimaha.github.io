Hi, I'm Ima - the person who makes these games.

The maker: Ima

Hi, I'm Ima. I make small iPhone games on my own. I like shogi (Japanese chess), poker and making apps. I plan, design, program and submit every app to the App Store myself.

Hello

My favorite kind of game is one you can play in a short break: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, so you can compare with other players fairly.

My first app, Make 10 Drive: License Plates, came out on October 7, 2026. There are now 16 apps on the App Store in Japan.

Games

Puzzles, relaxing games and versus games. The apps are in Japanese for now.

  • Puzzles · 5
  • Relaxing · 3
  • Versus · 4

What I care about

  • Short sessions you can play in a spare minute
  • No account or sign-in - just a nickname if you want to join a ranking
  • Collect as little data as possible, and keep records on your iPhone when we can

Contact

Bug reports, ideas and questions are welcome by email: imaimaha.apps@gmail.com. English or Japanese is fine.
