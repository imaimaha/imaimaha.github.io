What is Mental Math Runner: Gate Rush?

Mental Math Runner: Gate Rush is a simple running game where you do mental math on the move. Two math gates appear side by side - run through the one that makes your number bigger, and keep growing it all the way to the goal.

How to play

  • Tap the left or right half of the screen to choose a gate
  • + and × gates make your number grow; − and ÷ gates make it shrink
  • Reach the goal to clear the stage. The bigger your final number, the more stars you earn (up to 3 per stage)
  • If your number drops to 0 or below, you're out

200 stages

The first stages use small additions and subtractions. Later you'll meet ×, ÷, bigger numbers and "deadly gates". From stage 101 there are longer courses, ×6-×9 and ÷6-÷9 gates, tougher deadly gates and faster speeds.

  • Deadly gate: one side knocks you out instantly. Compare your number with the gate and pick the side that keeps you above 0
  • Taking the smaller gate early can leave you short when a big "−" gate comes later

Courses never change, so you can retry as often as you like and find the best route.

Daily stage

A very long course that changes every day at midnight Japan time - the same course for every player. It starts easy and gets harder and faster. See how far you can go and compare the number of gates you passed. You can also run past days' courses from a calendar (not ranked).

Ranking

Compare today's daily stage, this week's total and your total stars with all players, using just a nickname. No account or sign-in needed. Your nickname and records are sent only if you choose a nickname, and you can delete them from the ranking screen at any time.

Good to know

  • When you're out, you can watch a short video ad to continue once from that spot (optional). The daily ranking uses your record from before the revive
  • Pause or restart at any time with the pause button at the top left
  • Optional daily reminder at 7 pm when a new daily stage arrives (you can turn it off any time)
  • Banner ads appear on the title, stage select and result screens, never while you are running. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear when you leave the result screen
  • Vibration can be turned off
  • No login. Your progress is saved on your device. Anonymous play counts per stage (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
