What is Oto no Kaidan: Tap a Melody?

Every key you place plays the next note of the song. A music game where your taps perform the melody.

How to play

  • A round note bounces while moving left and right
  • Tap to make a key under it - and the next note of the song plays
  • Tap just above the dotted line for a "Perfect!" - a clear, clean tone, and your next key goes 2 levels up
  • Keys are colored by do-re-mi. The circle on the left shows the next note
  • Sheet music at the bottom shows the notes you played and the ones to come
  • Touch a black key and it's over - wait until the note is on the open side, then place your key to slip through

One song per stage

Play to the last note to clear a song. More Perfects give more stars and open the next song. 10 songs whose melodies are free for everyone: Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Jingle Bells, Fur Elise, Canon and more. The result screen shows your notes on a staff.

Medley

The 10 songs play one after another. Compare how many notes you played today, this week and all time (just choose a nickname).

Sounds and skins

Ball color, key shape (xylophone, bell, keyboard, bean), sound (piano, music box, marimba, glockenspiel, chiptune) and paper color. Unlocked by clearing songs, collecting stars and playing every day - never bought with ads or purchases.

Free with ads (never during play). Works offline; only the rankings use the network.
