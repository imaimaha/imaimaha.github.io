What is Shogi Variants: Play Online?

Shogi Variants: Play Online is shogi (Japanese chess) with a twist. Play variant rules online with friends, against the CPU, or with two players on one iPhone. It also includes 2250 variant tsume (checkmate) problems.

Play online with friends

  • Create a room and send the 6-character room code or an invite link to a friend. Your friend installs this free app and enters the code. No account or login
  • Private rooms are hidden from the room list; in public rooms anyone can send a join request
  • You play only with players you accept as the host
  • Time controls: none, sudden death, main time + byoyomi, byoyomi only, or Fischer
  • If the connection drops, return to the same room to continue
  • Review the game record afterwards (your last 5 online games are saved)

Rules

  • Annan, Anhoku, Anto and Anzai shogi: a piece borrows the moves of the friendly piece right behind it (or in front of it, or to its right or left, depending on the rule). 9×9 and 5×5 boards, with an optional "capture the king" setting
  • Card shogi: you can only move a piece on the file shown by the card you draw. When in check, you can move freely without using a card. Hold 0-3 cards, or draw first
  • Restriction shogi: regular shogi with handicaps such as "you can't enter the enemy camp until you've built a Mino castle" or "your king can't move until you're checked". Pick 1-3 restrictions from castles, openings and move rules. Moves that break them can't be played, and when you're in check you can play freely. Keep your opponent's restrictions secret until the end, try 12 challenges, or play it online
  • Effect card shogi: regular shogi with 13 one-time special cards, such as two moves in a row
  • Check shogi: the first player to give check wins
  • Decoy shogi: secretly choose one of your pieces as a decoy; capture your opponent's decoy to win. It stays hidden online too
  • Regular shogi

CPU with 10 levels

From 10-kyu to 5-dan, for beginners and experienced players alike. Choose to move first or second. In regular shogi, the CPU knows common openings (Yagura, Kakugawari, Shikenbisha, Nakabisha, Mino and Anaguma castles and more) and always finds a mate when there is one. Ratings are kept per rule.

2250 variant tsume problems

9×9 checkmate problems in the Annan, Anhoku, Anto and Anzai rules - you need the borrowed moves to solve them.

  • 1901 strict tsume problems (1 to 15 moves, beginner to very hard), plus practical mates and real-game positions
  • Solve on the board, or read the moves without moving the pieces
  • Choose the defender's replies yourself, or hide the number of moves
  • After solving, study other defenses in analysis mode
  • Tsume grading test (10-kyu to 7-dan)
  • 3 free hints per day across all problems; watch a short video for 3 more (optional)

Good to know

  • Everything except online play works offline
  • No login. Records stay on your iPhone. Online, only a short nickname is shown; names are filtered and can be reported or hidden
  • Banner ads appear on menus and CPU games, never during online games. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear after CPU games. Video ads are always optional (extra hints, answers or undos)
  • Sound and vibration can be turned off
