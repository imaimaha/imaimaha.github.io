What is Caterpillar: One Line Puzzle?

Caterpillar: One Line Puzzle is a simple one-stroke drawing puzzle. Drag from the caterpillar's head across the board and eat every leaf exactly once. Finish the board and the caterpillar becomes a chrysalis, then flies away as a butterfly.

How to play

  • Drag from the caterpillar's head to a neighboring leaf
  • Each leaf can be eaten only once. Eat them all to clear the stage
  • Took a wrong turn? Trace back along your path and continue from there, or start over
  • If you get stuck, a small guide offers a restart or a hint

200 stages in 20 chapters

Every chapter adds a new twist:

  • Stones: you can't pass them
  • Free start: on boards without a caterpillar, start from any leaf you like
  • Chrysalis branch: it must be the very last square
  • One-way leaves: you can only leave in the arrow's direction
  • Big leaves: visit them twice
  • Warp holes: holes of the same color are connected
  • Numbing mushrooms: after eating one, your next step must go straight
  • Berries: eat them in order, 1, 2, 3

From chapter 11 the boards grow up to 9×9, and each has exactly one solution. Chapters 1-11 are open from the start; later chapters unlock one by one. Each new twist is explained with a small picture card, and you can tap a special square to see its rule.

Stars

Clear faster to earn up to 3 stars per stage (the target times are shown on screen). Going back never costs stars.

Daily puzzle

A new board every day at midnight Japan time - the same board for every player, mixing all kinds of twists. Your first try goes to the ranking; after that you can practice as often as you like. Replay your route square by square, and play past days' puzzles too (not ranked). During your first 7 days, hints are available in the daily puzzle as well (+30 seconds each).

Ranking

Compare today's daily puzzle score and your total stars with all players, using just a nickname. No account or sign-in needed. Your nickname and records are sent only if you choose a nickname, and you can delete them from the ranking screen at any time.

Home screen widget

See at a glance whether you've solved today's puzzle, your streak, and the time until the next one. Tap it to jump straight to the daily puzzle.

Good to know

  • 3 free hints per day. After that, you can watch a short video ad to get another hint (optional - the game is fully playable without it)
  • Banner ads appear on the title, chapter select, stage select and clear screens, never while you are tracing. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear when you leave the clear screen
  • Vibration can be turned off
  • No login. Your stars and records are saved on your device. Anonymous play counts per stage (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
