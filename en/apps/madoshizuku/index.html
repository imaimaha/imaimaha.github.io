What is Window Drops: Relaxing Rain?

A rainy day. A car window. Blurry city lights outside.

Window Drops is a calm game where you gather raindrops with your finger and grow them bigger.

How to play

  • Trace the window to collect water into a drop
  • Bump it into other drops to merge and grow
  • Big drops are heavy - move too fast and they break apart
  • Drops that reach the bottom edge run off
  • Let go, and heavy drops slide down, leaving a trail

5 kinds of rain

Mist, fine drizzle, steady rain, heavy rain and a sudden downpour. The harder it rains, the faster drops grow - and the faster they run. The downpour blows sideways, with thunder. Your best is saved for each kind of rain.

3 modes

  • 60-second challenge: how big can you grow a drop in 60 seconds? Watch out for the wiper
  • Relaxed mode: no timer, no wiper. Watch the rainy window as long as you like
  • Sleep rain sounds: just watch the window while the rain sounds play. A 15-90 minute timer slowly fades them out. The screen stays on in this mode

Mix your own rain sounds

Rain, drops hitting the glass, thunder, a car engine and turn signal, and a music box - each with its own volume. All sounds are generated in the app.

New views outside the window

Grow bigger drops to unlock new scenes: night city, evening highway, tunnel, snowy night, neon streets, cherry blossoms at night, fireworks and more - 12 scenes in all. From December 1 to 25, the daily rain becomes a limited snowy window.

Things to collect

  • Postcards: make a drop larger than 11 mm to receive a postcard of the city reflected in it
  • Rainy-day diary: each day you play is saved with its rain, scene and biggest drop
  • Daily rain: a different rain and goal every day
  • Badges and 8 drop-size ranks in the drop collection

Ranking

Compare the biggest drop from your 60-second challenge with all players (this week / all time), plus your total merges. No account or sign-in needed. Your nickname and records are sent only if you choose a nickname, and you can delete them from the ranking screen at any time.

Good to know

  • Mute all sounds with the button at the top right
  • No connection needed to play. Ranking records are sent when you're online
  • Your records are saved on your device. Anonymous play counts (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
  • Banner ads appear on the title and result screens only - never during play or sleep rain sounds
  • Vibration can be turned off
