What is Poker Preflop Practice Quiz?

Poker Preflop Practice Quiz is a study app for Texas Hold'em decisions, one question at a time. Look at your two cards and your seat, decide whether to play and how, then check the reason and the range chart. Start with the first decision (preflop), and move on to flop, turn and river questions when you're ready.

All scores and points in the app are practice points for tracking your progress. They can't be bought, exchanged for anything or sent to anyone, and there are no in-app purchases.

Quiz practice

  • Choose what to practice: preflop, flop, turn & river, or a mix
  • Preflop: see the seat diagram, the earlier players' actions and your two cards, then choose fold, call or raise
  • Flop: as the preflop raiser, choose check, 33% or 75% of the pot; when an opponent leads, choose fold, call or raise
  • Turn & river: keep up the pressure or not, bluff-catching calls, and numbers like the equity you need to call and MDF
  • Four grades of feedback (correct / correct with a mixed hand / OK / miss), with a bar of action frequencies, a short reason and a 13×13 range chart

Fix your weak spots

  • Position practice: focus on one seat (UTG, HJ, CO, BTN, SB, BB)
  • Weak hands: hands you missed come back right away, then after 5 minutes, 1 hour and 1 day
  • Build a spot: choose the number of players (2-9), stack (10-100 BB), your seat and earlier actions to see a suggested range, and practice just that spot
  • Stats: overall accuracy plus accuracy by position, situation and board type, and a list of your weak hands

Practice tables vs CPU (offline)

  • Practice tournaments: choose 2 to 500 players, the starting stack and how fast the blinds rise, and play against CPU players only
  • Practice ring games: the blinds stay the same and play continues, against CPU players only
  • Every decision comes with an explanation that considers stack depth and how close you are to the final places, plus a range chart. Afterwards, a review shows what went well, what to focus on next, and each hand
  • Choose "preflop only" or "flop and beyond"
  • Fast fold: decide to fold early while others act, and still see the explanation during the next hand (not offered preflop in the big blind)
  • The action buttons always stay in the same place (fold, check/call, raise, all-in) to prevent mis-taps
  • Challenge mode: track your practice points across set tournaments and ring games. Practice points are only a progress score; if they run out, you can start over
  • 5 CPU levels: stronger CPU players follow the range charts more closely

Practice ranking

  • Compare your practice points in challenge mode and this week's correct quiz answers with other players, using a nickname
  • Only players who choose a nickname take part. Only the nickname and practice results are sent, and you can remove your records at any time

About the ranges (honestly)

The charts are simplified, easy-to-remember versions of commonly taught 6-max, 100 BB ranges. They are guidelines, not exact solver output. Answers for the flop and later streets are also guidelines from this app's own equity model.

Good to know

  • No login. Your records are saved on this iPhone (if you join the ranking, only your nickname and practice results are sent)
  • Banner ads appear at the bottom of the home, seat select, range chart and stats screens - never while you answer a question or play at a practice table. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear at a break point
  • Sound and vibration can be turned off in Settings
  • Anonymous play counts (no IDs or names) help us improve the questions; you can turn this off
