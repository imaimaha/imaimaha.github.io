What is Number Rooms: Rectangle Puzzle?

Number Rooms is a cozy logic puzzle about apartment floor plans. Use the numbers as clues to divide the grid into rooms. When a room is right, residents such as cats, ghosts, grandpas and robots move in and the furniture appears. Most puzzles take 30 seconds to 2 minutes - just right for a short break.

ROOM SPLIT (245 stages)

  • Divide the grid into rectangles (squares are OK)
  • Each room contains exactly one number, which equals the number of cells in that room
  • Drag to draw a room; tap a room to erase it
  • Grids from 5×5 to 11×11, across 15 floors

FIND THE RESIDENTS (120 stages)

  • Place residents in the colored rooms - as many as the room's number
  • Every row and every column has exactly one resident
  • Residents never touch each other, not even diagonally
  • Tap a cell to mark × (empty), tap again for a resident. Drag to mark many × at once
  • Grids from 5×5 to 11×11, across 10 floors

Easy to start

A short hands-on tutorial teaches each rule. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone - no guessing needed.

Hints that explain

A hint shows one room (or resident) that can be decided next and tells you why. If you have a wrong room, it points that out first.

Daily puzzles

One new Room Split and one new Find the Residents every day at midnight Japan time - the same puzzles for everyone. Your first try of the day goes to the ranking; after that, practice as much as you like. Replay your moves step by step, and play past days' puzzles too (not ranked).

Ranking

Compare today's times and your total stars with all players, using just a nickname. No account or sign-in needed. Your nickname and records are sent only if you choose a nickname, and you can delete them from the ranking screen at any time.

Home screen widget

See whether you've solved today's puzzles, your streak, and the time until the next ones.

Good to know

  • Clear faster to earn up to 3 stars per stage. Your best times are saved
  • 3 free hints per day. After that, you can watch a short video ad to get another hint (optional)
  • Banner ads appear on the title, stage select and result screens, never while you are solving. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear when you leave the result screen
  • Vibration can be turned off
  • No login. Your stars and records are saved on your device. Anonymous play counts per stage (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
