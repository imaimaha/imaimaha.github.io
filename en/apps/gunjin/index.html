What is Gunjin Shogi Online (Military Shogi)?

Gunjin Shogi ("military shogi") is a Japanese board game of hidden pieces and mind games. The app is the referee, so just two players can play.

In Gunjin Shogi, neither player knows what the other's pieces are. Normally a third person has to act as referee - here, the app does it. When your piece runs into an enemy piece, you are told only whether you won, lost or both were removed. Which piece looks strong? Where might the mines be? Read your opponent's setup from the results of each battle.

How to play

  • The board is 6 wide and 8 deep. The 4 rows on your side are your camp
  • First, place your 23 pieces in your camp. Pick a template (balanced, attack, defense or random), then tap two pieces to swap them
  • Most pieces move 1 square forward, back or sideways. Tanks and cavalry move 2 squares, engineers move any distance, and planes can jump over pieces forward and back. Mines and the flag can't move
  • When you move into an enemy piece, they battle. The stronger piece stays; if they're the same, both are removed
  • Win by moving an officer from major up to general into the enemy headquarters, or when your opponent has no pieces left to move

Pieces (23 pieces, 16 kinds)

General, lieutenant general, major general, colonel, lieutenant colonel, major, captain, first lieutenant, second lieutenant, plane, tank, cavalry, engineer, mine, spy and flag. The spy beats the general, and a mine loses only to planes and engineers. A battle chart with piece pictures shows which piece beats which at any time.

The app is the referee

  • Only your own pieces are shown. The type and position of enemy pieces never appear on screen
  • You only learn where a battle happened and whether you won, lost or drew
  • When the game ends, all enemy pieces are revealed

Ways to play

  • vs CPU: easy, normal or strong. The CPU also sees only its own pieces and the battle results - no cheating
  • 2 players on one iPhone: a hand-over screen appears every turn so you never see your opponent's pieces
  • Online: create a room and wait, or ask to join a waiting room - the game starts when the host accepts. Invite a friend to a private room with a 6-character code
  • Time controls: 3 or 10 minutes per player, 10 or 30 seconds per move, or 5 minutes + 5 seconds per move (CPU and 2-player games can also have no clock). The clock stops during setup
  • Say hi with 9 preset stamps during a game

Good to know

No account needed. Online, only a short nickname you choose is shown to your opponent, and you can report or hide names. Ads appear on the title and result screens, never during a game.
