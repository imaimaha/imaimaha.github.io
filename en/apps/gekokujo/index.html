What is Gekokujo Duel: 1 Beats 9?

Higher number wins. But 1 beats 9.

Gekokujo Duel is a two-player number card game about reading your opponent. ("Gekokujo" is a Japanese word for the lowly overthrowing the mighty.) You can learn the rules in 30 seconds, and the more you read your opponent, the deeper it gets.

How to play

  • Each player has one card of each number from 1 to 9
  • Each round, both players play one card face down. The higher number wins 1 point
  • But 1 beats 9 - gekokujo!
  • The winner of the previous round plays first; the other player plays after the first card is down
  • Used cards don't come back. You never see which numbers your opponent played - only who won each round
  • After 9 rounds, the player with more points wins

One-time special cards

  • Peek: when playing second, see the number on the first player's card (from round 2)
  • Double: the winner of this round gets 2 points
  • Guard: if you lose this round, it's a draw instead

Whether your opponent used a special card stays secret during the match, and so do the points - you only see rounds won.

When do you play your 9? Do you keep your 1 to catch their 9? Where do you use Double? Every match plays out differently.

Rank up to Meijin

Beat 10 rivals with different styles one by one, from 10-kyu "Tamago" up to Meijin (master). One loves Double, one loves Guard, a cat goes for the gekokujo... reading their habits is part of the strategy.

Daily challenge

A new challenge every day, like "Win with a gekokujo" or "Win without special cards". Keep your streak going, and play past days' challenges too.

Achievements & skins

15 achievements, such as your first gekokujo, a perfect win or a big comeback. They unlock new card backs (waves, cherry blossoms, checkers, starry sky, gold leaf and more) and table colors.

3 ways to play

  • vs CPU: ranked matches, the daily challenge and free play (easy, normal, strong)
  • 2 players on one iPhone: take turns, with a hand-over screen each round so your cards stay hidden
  • Online: create a room and accept join requests, or invite a friend to a private room with a 5-character code or invite link. No sign-up or login. Say hi with preset stamps

After the match

A review table shows every card both players played, where special cards were used, and the points. Share the result as an image.

Reading hints (CPU matches)

See which cards your opponent probably has left and your chance of winning with each of your cards. Once per match: free once a day, then by watching an optional short video.

Good to know

No login. CPU and 2-player games work offline, and your records are saved on your device. Ads appear on the title and result screens, never during a match. Online, only a short nickname is shown; names are filtered and can be reported or hidden.
