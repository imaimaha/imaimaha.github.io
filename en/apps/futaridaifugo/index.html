What is Futari Daifugo: 1v1 Daihinmin?

Daifugo (also called Daihinmin - a Japanese card game similar to President) for two players, one on one.

The 52 cards (no jokers) are split into red (hearts and diamonds) and black (spades and clubs), one color per player. Both hands have two of every number, so the deal is fair. You can't see your opponent's hand, but if you remember the cards played, you can work out what's left. When to use your strong cards and when to pass - it's a game of reading each other.

How to play

  • Card strength goes 3, 4, ... K, A, 2 (2 is the strongest)
  • Play one card, or 2-4 cards of the same number together
  • When there are cards on the table, play the same number of cards with a higher number, or pass
  • When your opponent passes, the table is cleared and the last player to play leads
  • The first player to empty their hand wins

Special cards

  • 5 Skip: skip your opponent's turn and play again
  • 7 Pass: give your opponent cards of your choice, as many as you played
  • 8 Cut: clear the table and lead again
  • 10 Discard: discard cards of your choice, as many as you played
  • Jack Back: playing a J reverses card strength until the table is cleared
  • Revolution: four of the same number reverse card strength (7 Pass moves cards around, so four of a kind can happen)

Cards you can't finish with

Your very last play can't be the strongest number (usually 2), a 5, 7, 8, 10 or J, or a revolution. Use your special cards early!

Modes

  • vs CPU: easy, normal or strong. One game decides the match
  • Online: create a room and wait, or send a join request to a waiting room; the game starts when the host accepts. Invite friends to a private room with a 5-character code or an invite link
  • Preset stamps (10 kinds) during play

Good to know

  • The rules are always available in the app (on the title screen, and the "?" button during play)
  • No login. Online, only a short nickname is shown; names are filtered and can be reported or hidden
  • Banner ads appear on the title and result screens, never during play. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear after CPU games
  • Anonymous play counts (no IDs or names) help us improve; you can turn this off
