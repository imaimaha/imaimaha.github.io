What is Nazotte 10: Math Dungeon Puzzle?

Trace numbers to make exactly 10. A puzzle RPG where addition defeats monsters.

Trace the number tiles 1-9 on the board in one stroke without lifting your finger. When the total is exactly 10, the tiles vanish and hit the enemy. A 10 made from 2 tiles or from 5 tiles both count - the more tiles you use, the stronger the attack.

How to play

  • Trace from tile to tile - up, down, left, right or diagonal
  • When the total is exactly 10, the tiles clear and you attack
  • When the gauge around an enemy's face fills up, it does the "next move" shown on screen
  • Time stops until you clear your first 10 and after each enemy move, so you can look at the board calmly
  • There is always at least one 10 you can make on the board
  • Stuck? Use a magnifying glass item to see a hint

Special tiles

  • Star: double damage
  • Shield: blocks an enemy attack
  • Heart: restores HP
  • Rock: breaks when you make a 10 next to it

8 chapters to explore

Forest path, mountain cave, night castle, rocky shore, snowy mountain road, sand ruins, sky island and the candy land. Each chapter has new enemies and terrain, with a boss waiting at the end - plus shops, rest stops and treasure chests along the way.

Get stronger

  • Earn stars from defeated enemies and cleared chapters, and use them to raise max HP, attack, defense and more
  • Defeated enemies can join you as companions, each with one special skill
  • Clearing a chapter can give you an item - equip one before you set out
  • Your hero levels up with every adventure
  • Open mystery boxes with medals to get companions, items and new hero faces
  • Slow the enemy gauge with Time, hit harder with Power, or protect yourself with Guard. Find a combination for the harder chapters

Monster book

Every monster you defeat goes into the book. Collect the cute, slightly creepy monsters.

Good to know

  • No account needed. Your records are saved on your iPhone
  • Ads appear at the bottom of screens such as the title and map, never during battles
  • When you lose, you can watch an optional video to revive once
