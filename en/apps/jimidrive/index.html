What is Make 10 Drive: License Plates?

Can you make 10 from the license plate of a passing car?

Make 10 Drive is a mental math game set on a Japanese expressway at night. Oncoming cars show 4-digit license plates, and you decide in a few seconds whether those four numbers can make 10 - the classic "make ten" puzzle people in Japan play with train tickets and car plates.

How to play

  • The plate of each passing car is shown large
  • Use each of the 4 digits once, with + − × ÷ and parentheses, to try to make 10
  • Tap one of two buttons: "I can make 10" or "Impossible"
  • Correct answers bring you closer to your destination. After each answer, the app shows a formula that makes 10

More modes

  • Prime mode: is the 4-digit number a prime?
  • Mix: each plate asks either "make 10?" or "prime?"
  • Time attack: race to your destination against the clock
  • Endless: keep driving, turning around at the end of the line, until you run out of misses
  • Choose 1, 3 or 5 allowed misses

Daily drive & ranking

One course that changes every day: every player gets the same plates in the same order. Only your first run of the day counts for the ranking. Compare today's rank and this week's total with all players, plus a ranking for the number of places you've reached. Only the nickname you choose and your records appear. You can also replay past days' drives from a calendar (not ranked).

Across Japan, starting from Tokyo

At first you can only start from Tokyo. Each destination you reach becomes a new starting point: reach Haneda for the bayshore route, Yokohama for the Tomei Expressway, Nagoya to head for Kansai, and enough places to unlock Hokkaido. The time of day (night, evening, morning, daytime) follows your clock.

Local notes & collection

Reaching a destination shows a short note about local food, sights or the origin of the place name, and little bits of trivia appear along the way. Car types you've solved (sedans, kei cars, taxis, trucks, patrol cars, classic cars and more), local plates, special numbers like repeated digits, places reached and notes all go into your collection.

Good to know

  • No login. Your records (bests, collection, unlocked places) are saved on your device; your nickname and records are sent only if you join the ranking
  • Banner ads appear on the title and menu screens, never while you are driving. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear when you leave the result screen
  • When you run out of misses, you can watch a short video ad to continue once (optional)
  • Optional daily reminder at 7 pm when a new daily drive arrives
  • Vibration can be turned off. Anonymous play counts (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
