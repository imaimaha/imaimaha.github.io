What is Natto Peel: Sticky Strings?

That thin film on a pack of natto at breakfast - can you peel it off cleanly?

Natto Peel is a 3D time-attack game: peel the film off a pack of natto (Japanese fermented soybeans) and wind up every sticky string as fast as you can.

How to play

  • Pinch the glowing corner and lift slowly to peel off the film
  • Once it's off, lift it high to pull the strings tight
  • Move in circles to wind the strings up
  • Pull too hard and strings snap - that's a time penalty. Beans stuck to the film cost time too

10 kinds of natto

Small bean, chopped (hikiwari), large bean, black bean, straw-wrapped, green soybean, extra-large, snow-chopped and more. Each kind is different in how the strings form, how easily they snap and how easy they are to wind. Play more to meet new kinds and fill your natto collection.

More ways to play

  • Daily natto: a different kind every day (midnight Japan time). Sometimes it's one you haven't met yet
  • 3-pack streak: peel three packs in a row for a total time
  • Ranks S to C and 12 achievements

Ranking

  • Daily score: everyone peels the same natto of the day. Play as many times as you like - your best score of the day is ranked
  • Fastest single-pack time (adjusted for each kind's difficulty), this week and all time, plus total strings wound up

Compare with all players using just a nickname. No account or sign-in needed. Your nickname and records are sent only if you choose a nickname, and you can delete them from the ranking screen at any time.

Good to know

  • Pause, restart or go back to the title any time with the pause button at the top right
  • Banner ads appear on the title and result screens, never while you are playing. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear after a result
  • Vibration can be turned off
  • No login. Your records are saved on your device. Anonymous play counts (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
