What is 3D Four in a Row Online?

Across, down, up and diagonal. A two-player game of four in a row - in 3D.

16 poles stand on a 4×4 board. Red and blue take turns dropping one ball at a time. Balls stack from the bottom, up to 4 on each pole. Line up 4 of your color in a straight line to win. There are 76 possible lines in all, including diagonals on each level and diagonals through the whole cube - some of them are easy to miss from any angle.

How to play

  • Tap a pole to select it, then tap again to drop the ball to the bottom (so you don't drop one by mistake). You can switch to one-tap dropping in Settings
  • Rotate the board by swiping or with the rotate buttons, or view it from almost straight above
  • The last ball played has a glowing ring, and a winning line lights up
  • Careful: if you play on a pole below your opponent's winning spot, they can drop right on top of it

Ways to play

  • vs CPU: easy, normal or strong. Play first, second or random, and take back moves to your own turn
  • 2 players on one iPhone: take turns
  • Online: create a room and wait, or ask to join a waiting room - the game starts when the host accepts. Invite a friend to a private room with a 6-character code. Time limits: none, 30 seconds per move, or 3 minutes per player
  • Say hi with 9 preset stamps during a game

Records

Wins, losses and draws for each CPU level, your best winning streak, your fastest win and your online record.

Good to know

No account needed. Online, only a short nickname you choose is shown to your opponent, and you can report or hide names. Ads appear on the title and result screens, never during a game.
