What is Boiled Egg Peeling: Tsurutto?

Just the shell - smooth and clean.

Tsurutto is a 3D game all about peeling a boiled egg perfectly.

How to play

  • Tap the shell to crack it (tap too much and...)
  • Drag from a crack to peel. Too fast or a sharp turn, and you'll tear the white
  • Drag on an empty area, or use two fingers, to turn the egg
  • Peel off all the shell to finish. You're ranked SSS to F by time and how clean the white is

18 eggs to collect

White, brown, quail, silkie, cherry-blossom dyed, Easter egg, dinosaur egg, ostrich, century-egg style, golden, moon, a rare egg with something inside, blue, cow-print, onsen egg, chocolate-colored, black and ice. Each egg has its own number of shell pieces, hardness and fragility. The more you peel, the more eggs you meet.

More ways to play

  • Daily egg: a different egg every day (midnight Japan time). Sometimes it's a rare egg you haven't met yet
  • Extra-hard soft-boiled mode: the white tears easily, and if you dig too deep you'll see the yolk
  • Once in a while, there's a surprise inside...
  • From December 1 to 25, the daily egg becomes a limited Christmas egg

Ranking

  • Daily score: everyone peels the same egg of the day. Play as many times as you like - your best score of the day is ranked
  • Smoothness score (this week / all time), flawless time (the fastest peel without damaging the white), and total eggs peeled

Compare with all players using just a nickname. No account or sign-in needed. Your nickname and records are sent only if you choose a nickname, and you can delete them from the ranking screen at any time.

One more chance

If the egg explodes or the white crumbles, you can watch a video ad to continue that egg once (optional; continued eggs are not ranked).

Good to know

  • Pause, restart or go back to the title any time with the pause button at the top left
  • Banner ads appear on the title and result screens, never while you are peeling. A full-screen ad may occasionally appear when you leave the result screen
  • Vibration can be turned off
  • No login. Your records are saved on your device. Anonymous play counts (no IDs or names) help us tune difficulty; you can turn this off
