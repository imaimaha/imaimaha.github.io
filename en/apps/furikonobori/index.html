What is Pendulum Climb?

Swing, hook and climb higher. A pendulum game you play with just a tap.

How to play

  • A glowing ball hangs from a point on a string and swings
  • Tap to let go and hook onto the nearest higher point within reach. Points you can reach are shown with a faint dotted line
  • Hook on while swinging upward with speed, and you'll shoot up
  • Tap when no point is in reach and you let go and fall
  • Glowing mist rises from below. Touch it and it's over

Past landmarks, into space, to the Moon

The view changes as you climb: city buildings, mountains, above the clouds, the starry sky and the blue Earth. Pass Tokyo Tower (333 m), Tokyo Skytree (634 m), Mt. Fuji (3,776 m), the stratosphere, space (100 km), the International Space Station and finally the Moon (384,400 km). A message pops up each time you pass a landmark, and "Next: Mt. Fuji in ○ m" always shows your next goal.

Skins you unlock by playing

Choose the ball color, trail, string, point shape and background (27 in all). New skins unlock as you pass landmarks, climb a lot and play every day. They can't be bought with ads or money.

Pick your music

The original music is generated in the app. On "Auto" the tune changes with your height, or choose gentle, upbeat, night or space - or no music. Sound effects and vibration can each be turned off.

Rankings

Compare your best height today, this week and all time. No sign-in - just choose a nickname. Nothing is sent unless you choose one, and you can delete your records any time.

Good to know

  • Your records and skins are saved on your iPhone. No connection needed to play (only the rankings go online)
  • Banner ads appear on the title and result screens, never during play. After a few games, a full-screen ad sometimes appears on the result screen
  • When you fall, you can watch an optional video ad to continue once from where you fell
